# Launch Distribution Plan

## Target Channels
- **Reddit**: r/relationships, r/tech, r/relationshipadvice
- **Twitter/X**: Use hashtags #relationshipadvice, #techtools, #digitalprivacy
- **LinkedIn**: Target dating advice and tech communities

## Timeline
- **Week 1**: Finalize email setup and analytics tracking
- **Week 2**: Launch on Reddit and Twitter with targeted posts
- **Week 3**: Monitor engagement and adjust strategy based on feedback

## Content Strategy
- **Reddit**: Post detailed guides on using the app for relationship analysis
- **Twitter**: Share short tips and engage with relevant hashtags
- **LinkedIn**: Share professional insights on digital privacy and relationship tech

## Metrics to Track
- Engagement rates on Reddit and Twitter
- Click-through rates on launch posts
- Initial user signups from distribution channels
