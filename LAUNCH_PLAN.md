# Launch Distribution Plan (§2.10)

Per OUTSTANDING_WORK.md §2.10: timed to launch after §4 (real billing) ships,
so a one-shot attention spike doesn't hit an app that can't yet take payment.

## Target channels
- Reddit: r/relationships, r/dating_advice, r/socialskills
- X/Twitter: relationship-advice and dating-tech communities
- Product Hunt

## Timeline (once §4 ships)
- Week 1: finalize email + analytics, confirm funnel events are flowing
- Week 2: launch on Reddit and X with targeted posts
- Week 3: monitor engagement, iterate on messaging

## Metrics to track
- Engagement rate on launch posts
- Click-through rate into the app
- Signup and first-audit completion rate from each channel
