### Landing Page → Blog Posts

- Use 2-3 recent articles as featured links
- Contextual anchor text per article topic

### Blog Posts → Product Pages
Every blog post should include:
1. Direct link to `/signup` CTA with benefit-focused anchor text
2. Link to `/pricing` or `/comparison` if relevant
3. Internal link to related blog posts (manual curation at launch scale)
